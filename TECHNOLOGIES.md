# Technology Proficiencies

This document outlines my proficiency levels with various backend, frontend, database, and other relevant technologies. My proficiency is categorized as:

*   **Proficient:** I can confidently use these technologies for development, understand their core concepts well, and can typically troubleshoot common issues.
*   **Somewhat Proficient:** I have a working knowledge of these technologies. I can use them for simpler tasks and may need to consult documentation or examples for more complex scenarios.
*   **Not Proficient (requires learning/external tools):** I have limited to no direct experience with these technologies. I would require significant learning, or tasks involving them might be better suited for external tools or specialists. My ability to learn is strong, however.
